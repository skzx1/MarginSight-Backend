# LINE Thailand commerce fees — seller-side specification

**Snapshot:** 24 October 2026 · **Market/currency:** Thailand / THB. The word LINE covers distinct storefront, discovery, messaging, payment and ordering products; fee rules must not be transferred from one product to another. “Official data not found” means the public official pages reviewed do not establish a value; it is not proof a charge is zero.

## Active seller-facing product map

Product pages: [MyShop](https://lineforbusiness.com/th/service/myshop), [LINE MINI Eats](https://lineforbusiness.com/th/service/lineminieats), [LINE MINI App](https://developers.line.biz/en/services/line-mini-app/), and [MyShop API](https://lineforbusiness.com/th/service/myshop/feature/myshop-api).

## Executive summary

## Five-line executive summary

1. **All-in range:** no universal MyShop/LINE take-rate; the 3% transaction fee and conditional 3% service fee apply to different order populations/bases.
2. **Typical conditional case:** if both fees apply to equal ฿1,000 bases, total is 6% + 7% VAT = ฿64.20; this is not a rate on every MyShop order.
3. **Campaign relief:** qualifying new sellers may get 0% transaction fee for 30 days, capped at 100 orders or ฿100,000 GMV, while the 3% service fee remains.
4. **Separate product:** LINE MINI Eats uses a promotional one-branch subscription of ฿990/month before VAT through 31 Mar 2027 and states 0% per-order GP.
5. **Production rule:** verify seller eligibility, LINE SHOPPING attribution, payment route and actual settlement; direct-bank-transfer promo dates conflict and post-shipment fee reversals are not specified publicly.

## Fee formulas and worked cases

For eligible MyShop orders only:

```text
transaction_fee = eligible_payment_base * 0.03   # or 0 within campaign limits
service_fee = eligible_attributed_base * 0.03   # only when attribution conditions apply
VAT = (transaction_fee + service_fee) * 0.07
fee_total = transaction_fee + service_fee + VAT
seller_net = seller receipts - fees/tax - seller-funded discounts - shipping/other adjustments
```

The Thai fee policy says fee amounts including VAT are rounded to two decimal places: if the third decimal is 0.0050 or greater, round up; otherwise round down. Source: [Thai policy](https://terms2.line.me/lineshopping_terms_and_conditions_fees_policy?lang=th). Apply after calculating each fee as specified in the fee policy; confirm the posted amount against the statement.

1. **Ordinary eligible order:** assume ฿1,000 verified base for each fee, completed and marked shipped, LINE SHOPPING-attributed. Payment fee = 30.00; service fee = 30.00; VAT = 4.20; total = **฿64.20**. This is conditional, not a universal take-rate: many orders have only one fee or neither.
2. **Seller + platform coupons / published policy illustration:** item ฿500; seller item coupon ฿50; LINE SHOPPING coupon ฿50; shipping ฿40; seller shipping discount ฿10; LINE POINTS ฿200; buyer payment ฿230. Thai policy formula: transaction base `230+200=430` → fee ฿12.90; service base `230+50+200=480` → fee ฿14.40; VAT ฿1.91; total fee **฿29.21**. The English translation instead prints `(290+200) × 3%` for both formulas, inconsistent with the fee amounts; use Thai policy formula and validate against settlement.
3. **Eligible new-seller campaign:** assume qualifying seller within first 100 orders, below ฿100,000 GMV cap and within 30-day benefit; both normal bases assumed ฿1,000, with attribution. Transaction fee 0; service fee 30; VAT 2.10; total **฿32.10**. Campaign waives only transaction fee; seller must still satisfy all criteria.
4. **Return/cancellation after shipping:** if a fee was posted and the order is later returned, public policy does not specify reversal timing or partial refund arithmetic. Set expected reversal as `UNVERIFIED`; keep original debit and actual credit/debit adjustment separate. A pre-shipment cancellation that never meets completed/Mark-as-Shipped trigger is not a qualifying fee order under the policy wording.

## Confidence, conflicts and verification steps

**Confirmed from official published pages:** 3% transaction fee and conditional 3% service fee; 7% VAT; policy amendment dates (1 Sep 2023; 16 Oct 2023; 15 Nov 2023; 10 Sep 2024); 2026 transaction-fee waiver conditions; MINI Eats single-branch promotional subscription/no per-order GP; MyShop API key/webhook limits.

**Conflict / unresolved:** direct bank-transfer promotion end date (30 Sep vs 31 Dec 2026 across official pages); post-shipment return/refund fee reversals; current OA subscription rates; standalone LINE Pay and MINI App merchant charges; shipping/COD/return carrier tariffs; API schema and fee fields.
