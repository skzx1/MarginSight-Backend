# Lazada Thailand seller-fee specification

**Snapshot:** 24 October 2026 · **Currency:** THB · **Scope:** domestic Thailand Marketplace (general seller) and LazMall. This is a research specification, not a binding seller tariff. Seller Center login-only fee schedules, enrollment terms, and settlement statements prevail. “Official” below means Lazada/its Open Platform; “secondary” means independent or third-party reporting. Where the public official page did not expose the fee table body, that limitation is explicit rather than treating a search snippet as a fully inspected schedule.

## Executive findings and source quality

The dependable public picture is: a completed-order Marketplace Service Fee (MSF/commission), a payment/transaction fee, and optional/program-dependent fees. Public sources disagree on VAT presentation, program bundling and some rates. The current official LazMall join page displays headline commission of **17.5%** and payment fee of **3.21%** (VAT-inclusive wording), while also presenting a 6–13% category table; no effective date or clear reconciliation was exposed. The official Seller Center has fee-table/program notices updated **6 May 2026** and a fee/Premium Package change notice dated **25 June 2026**, but accessible page bodies did not reveal a complete current leaf-category schedule. Therefore exact Marketplace and LazMall rates remain seller/category-specific and unverified from a complete official tariff.

DataGlass Labs reports that the official Lazada schedule effective **1 August 2026** is VAT-exclusive and specifies category-cluster ranges, with VAT added; KhumMai independently reports the same-date VAT-inclusive endpoints and selected leaf rates. UpscaleRank likewise reports those same endpoints, but these fee blogs are not independent official authority and likely share source material. Rates below are consequently marked secondary where applicable, not “official verified.” [DataGlass Labs, marketplace fee dataset](https://www.dataglasslabs.com/research/marketplace-fees) · [KhumMai, Lazada fee guide](https://khummai.com/lazada-fees) · [UpscaleRank](https://upscalerank.com/lazada-fees-thailand/) · [Official fee FAQ (10 Aug 2026 indexed)](https://sellercenter.lazada.co.th/helpcenter/s/faq/knowledge?categoryId=1000028085&hybrid=1&language=en-US&m_station=BuyerHelp&questionId=1000147680) · [LazMall joining page](https://sellercenter.lazada.co.th/apps/register/lazmall/joining)

## Five-line executive summary

1. **All-in fee range:** not defensibly quantifiable from public evidence; official general-seller leaf rates and program bases are not fully accessible.
2. **Official Mall-page headline:** 17.5% commission + 3.21% payment fee are displayed with VAT-inclusive wording; their bases and relation to the page’s 6–13% category table are not reconciled.
3. **Secondary-only signals:** third parties report 1 Aug 2026 category clusters and 8.56% VAT-inclusive Premium Package; these are not production-verified official tariffs.
4. **Conditional headline arithmetic:** 17.5% + 3.21% = 20.71% only if both charges apply to the same base; this is not a verified all-in Lazada take-rate.
5. **Production rule:** confirm seller tier, leaf category, Premium Package/enrollment, shipping and tax lines in Seller Center/settlement before using any example.

## Formula specification (use as provisional until matched to settlement)

Let `P` = listed item price, `SD` = seller-funded item discount, `PD` = platform-funded discount, `S_b` = buyer-paid shipping, `r_msf` = applicable category/tier MSF rate. The official LazMall page displays `r_pay = 0.0321` on item price, but the general-seller rate and authoritative fee base are unresolved.

- Provisional commission basis: `B_msf = P − SD` (platform discount treatment not verified); `MSF = B_msf × r_msf`.
- Official Mall payment-fee case, if applicable: use the current Seller Center base as given (public join page displays 3.21% × item price, but reconcile with settlement); do not infer shipping or voucher treatment from a secondary article.
- Provisional payment basis reported by secondary guides: `B_pay = (P − SD) + S_b`; `PaymentFee = B_pay × 0.0321`. This is not adopted as official; do not use until the seller’s current policy confirms it.
- Provisional Premium Package basis: `B_prog = P − SD`; `ProgramFee = B_prog × r_prog`, where reported 1 Aug 2026 `r_prog = 0.0856` VAT-inclusive. Confirm inclusion/eligibility before charging this in a model.
- If rate quoted pre-VAT: `fee incl. VAT = base × rate × 1.07`; if inclusive already, **do not multiply again**.
- Expected settlement before COGS, seller-paid freight, ads, affiliate, returns and taxes: buyer product/shipping payment + any reimbursed platform subsidy − seller discount − MSF − payment fee − enrolled program fees − logistics/adjustment lines. Settlement statement is authoritative; do not reconstruct from product price alone.
- No official Thai rounding rule found; preserve full precision until matched to the seller’s Finance statement and tax invoice.

These formulas are explicitly **inferred from secondary sources**, not represented as fully verified official Lazada rules. [KhumMai] [DataGlass] [Official fee FAQ]

## Worked examples (illustrative, not a promised payout)

Amounts are computed from the provisional formulas above. Rates are VAT-inclusive secondary examples; VAT is not added again. Platform voucher funding is set to zero because its exact settlement treatment is unknown.

1. **Marketplace, general merchandise example:** item price ฿1,000; seller discount ฿100; buyer-paid shipping ฿40; assumed applicable MSF **16.05%** (reported VAT-inclusive selected general categories); no optional program. Net item base ฿900. MSF = 900 × 16.05% = **฿144.45**. Payment fee = (900 + 40) × 3.21% = **฿30.17**. Fees total **฿174.62**; remainder from item and buyer shipping receipts = 940 − 174.62 = **฿765.38**, before seller-paid shipping, COGS, ads, affiliate, returns/tax adjustments. Rate/category must be checked against actual leaf SKU.
2. **Marketplace, fashion example:** item price ฿1,200; seller discount ฿0; buyer shipping ฿0; illustrative MSF **17.12% (secondary-only example; conflicts with the current official LazMall headline 17.5%)**. MSF = **฿205.44**; payment fee = **฿38.52**; total **฿243.96**; remainder **฿956.04** before other costs. This demonstrates why a Marketplace-wide flat commission is unsafe.
3. **LazMall + reported Premium Package example:** item ฿2,000; seller discount ฿0; buyer shipping ฿60; illustrative MSF **18.73%**, program **8.56%**. MSF **฿374.60**; payment fee `(2,000+60)×3.21%` = **฿66.13**; program fee **฿171.20**; total **฿611.93**; remainder from ฿2,060 receipts **฿1,448.07**. The 18.73% rate may not fit every Mall category; package enrollment/bundling and base must be checked in Seller Center.

**Return/cancel example:** two items at ฿250 each; one is returned and the other retained. Provisional rule from secondary sources: reverse the fee on the returned item and retain fees on the kept ฿250 item only; assuming a 16.05% MSF and no shipping, retained-item MSF = ฿40.125 and payment fee = ฿8.025, total ฿48.15 (rounded once to two decimals; independently rounded line items sum to ฿48.16). If both items/order are fully cancelled/returned, secondary sources say both fees reverse (฿0 net fee), but official reversal timing, shipping charges and refund adjustment are **not verified**. Match the actual adjustment lines rather than applying this as a guaranteed rule.

## Campaign calendar and seller tiers

- No reliable public official 2026 day-by-day submission calendar, cutoff time, campaign fee table, or universal voucher obligation was found. Campaigns are invitation/slot-specific; use the live Seller Center campaign registration screen and the campaign-specific terms. Do not use third-party claims of standard Pay Day/Mega Sale/11.11 discounts or CPC multipliers as official facts.
- “Marketplace” versus “LazMall” is the seller distinction in the fee schedules. Additional seller programs (including Premium Package/Top Seller) are enrollment-based; rates shown in secondary reporting may change by date and category. Account-specific/negotiated arrangements may differ.
- 2026 reported fee changes: 1 May (reported MSF/program adjustment), 25 June (another reported change), and 1 August (reported current MSF clusters and Premium Package 8% pre-VAT). These dates and figures are secondary-reported, not verified against accessible notice body. A public secondary dataset states 1 August superseded 25 June. Another secondary report says a September 2026 competition-authority measure froze platform fee rises for six months; the claim and legal scope were not verified in a primary government source, so **do not treat as settled fee law**. [DataGlass] [KhumMai fee updates](https://khummai.com/fee-updates) [PostToday](https://www.posttoday.com/smart-sme/741803)

## API mapping and extraction

Official Open Platform page documents `GET /finance/transaction/details/get`, operation **QueryTransactionDetails**, “Authorization Required,” description: query seller transaction details within a date range. Listed fields include `fee_type` (transaction type ID), `details` (transaction details), `seller_sku`, `lazada_sku`, `amount` (total transaction value), `VAT_in_amount` (VAT in amount), `WHT_amount`, and `WHT_included_in_amount`; use the page’s full current schema rather than assuming this is an exhaustive response. The page’s indexed “Latest update” is **28 July 2022**, so the schema may be stale. Thailand service endpoint from official Open Platform logistics documentation: `https://api.lazada.co.th/rest`. The finance detail page lists authorization as required but the retrieved documentation did **not** expose a named OAuth scope/permission string; report scope as **not found**, not guessed. [QueryTransactionDetails official docs](https://open.lazada.com/apps/doc/api?path=%2Ffinance%2Ftransaction%2Fdetails%2Fget) · [Official API endpoint region table](https://open.lazada.com/apps/doc/api?path=%2Flogistics%2Fepis%2Festimate_shipping_fee)

## Explicit unknowns / seller verification checklist

1. Current official SKU leaf-category table for both Marketplace and LazMall; whether a newly published platform restriction/freeze changed implementation.
2. Exact MSF and payment-fee treatment of platform-funded/co-funded discounts, voucher stacking, bundle discounts, partial refunds, shipping subsidy, and cash-on-delivery.
3. Which 1 Aug program benefits are bundled; whether legacy enrolled sellers are grandfathered or separately billed.
4. Campaign-specific rates, calendar, eligibility, submission cutoffs, seller-funded voucher obligations and VAT.
5. Current Thailand COD, reverse logistics, fulfillment/storage, remote-area, failed delivery, overweight and return-to-sender tariffs.
6. Current tax invoice and WHT base/rates for each fee and seller legal form; tax treatment must follow Lazada invoices and official Revenue Department guidance.
7. Listing/subscription/withdrawal/account fees and whether universal zeros apply to all service add-ons/account types.
9. API current schema, fee type dictionary, rate limit, date-window behavior, scope string, signs and currency; the specific QueryTransactionDetails page states an older update date (28 July 2022), while the Finance API overview is marked updated **19 July 2024**. Treat the fields as provisional until current schema and seller response are validated; the Thailand API host is not independently verified.

**Operational source of truth:** Seller Center Finance → Account Statement → transaction details; inspect the seller’s exact SKU category, program enrollment, campaign terms, tax invoice, and payout adjustments. Public research did not justify filling the above unknowns with guessed values.
