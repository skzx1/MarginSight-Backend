# Shopee Thailand seller-fee specification
**Snapshot:** 24 October 2026 · THB · Thailand domestic marketplace · Standard (Non-Mall) and Mall/Official sellers

## Executive finding
Shopee’s current public fee policy is Help Center article 77254, revised **3 July 2026**, with the sale-fee schedule explicitly changed effective **4 August 2026, 00:00 Thailand time**. It defines payment, platform infrastructure, account-maintenance, sale, installment and Special SPayLater charges, including bases and VAT treatment. It links category schedules as Google Drive files; the schedule rows were not independently normalized here. Therefore exact category-specific sale rates are **UNVERIFIED** and must be looked up in the linked sheet/PDF or seller settlement—not invented. Official page: https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77254

## Five-line executive summary

1. **All-in fee range:** no defensible single range; category sale rates, campaign/service enrollment, shipping and return adjustments are seller/order-specific or unavailable.
2. **Known standard-order charges:** 3% transaction fee + 7% VAT on the stated payment base, and ฿1 infrastructure fee + VAT per eligible successful order; not a complete take-rate.
3. **Commission:** category/tier formula is `(starting item price − seller-funded discount) × rate`; schedule effective 4 Aug 2026, but exact category rows remain unnormalized. Eligible small shops at ≤฿10,000 prior-30-day sales may get a 0.5–1.5% sale-fee discount; exact eligibility/tier is unresolved.
4. **Typical illustration:** ฿1,000 item + ฿40 buyer shipping gives ฿34.454 raw payment/PIF subtotal; illustrative whole-baht rounding yields ฿34, before sale fee, seller shipping, campaigns, ads or affiliate.
5. **Production rule:** use seller-specific fee schedule, program terms and escrow/settlement statement; Mall fee VAT wording remains conflicting and unresolved.

## Evidence and status convention
**Official** = directly stated in current Shopee policy/official seller or developer documentation; **official but inaccessible/unverified** = official link/search record exists but contents unavailable or page is gated; **secondary** = non-Shopee source (not used for rates); **inferred** = arithmetic or interpretation clearly identified; **unverified** = no authoritative numeric term found. Publication/revision date is distinguished from effective date. Where policy leaves rounding, settlement is authoritative.

## Category coverage (the requested 11 buckets)
1. Commission/referral: sale transaction fees above (Mall and Non-Mall schedules) and AMS affiliate (optional, terms-specific).
2. Transaction/payment: payment transaction, installment card, Special SPayLater.
3. Services/programs: PIF, account maintenance, PRIME/free-shipping programs, Seller Coins and optional services; no universal public program charge beyond PIF.
4. Campaign fees/requirements: event-specific and Seller Centre gated; no universal fee found.
5. Vouchers/discount funding/base: seller-funded item discount lowers sale-fee base; Shopee-funded PRM/Coins enter payment-fee base; shipping/other discounts excluded from sale-fee base. Voucher funding attribution and per-campaign co-funding proportions must be obtained from order settlement.
6. Shipping, returns, COD, fulfillment: separate, service/parcel-dependent; no single current rate card validated.
7. Affiliate: AMS seller-paid commission versus Seller Affiliate/Social Partners seller-received compensation are distinct.
8. Advertising: optional, spend/budget based; no order take-rate established.
9. Refund/return/cancel/penalties: policy potentially waives specified fees; acceptance/time window and ledger reversal are essential. Penalties not quantified.
10. VAT/WHT: clear VAT split in current fee policy except Mall conflict; WHT seller-specific/unverified.
11. Listing/subscription/withdrawal/account: only inactive-account ฿20/mo verified; other universal fees not found.

## Formula specification (auditable; do not blend unlike bases)
For each order, retain raw order/escrow and statement lines; determine category, seller tier, payment method, promotion funding, fulfillment, status and enrollment first.

- `sale_base = max(0, starting_item_price - seller_funded_item_discount)` (per policy; per-SKU allocation required for multi-item orders).
- `sale_fee = apply_current_account_discount(category_rate[tier, category, effective_date], eligible_smallshop_discount, sale_base)`. The policy describes a 0.5–1.5% small-shop sale-fee discount for shops at or below ฿10,000 in preceding 30-day sales, but the exact tier/eligibility application is not normalized here; obtain actual Seller Center rate before calculating.
- `payment_base = buyer_item_amount_payable_ex_shipping + buyer_paid_shipping + Shopee_Item_PRM + Shopee_Coins`. This separates goods amount from shipping so shipping is counted exactly once. In the examples, “Buyer payable” means checkout total including shipping; the payment base starts from goods payable, adds shipping, then adds Shopee PRM/Coins. Exclude seller-funded discount only once (it is already reflected in buyer goods payable); do not subtract it a second time. Reconcile the exact meaning of each API amount to order fields.
- `payment_fee_exVAT = 0.03 * payment_base`; `VAT = 0.07 * payment_fee_exVAT`; payment fee billed = sum.
- Installment card/SPayLater: substitute payment-method-specific rate and corresponding paid amount/shipping base; do **not** stack standard 3% separately because the policy describes those rates as inclusive of the base transaction component.
- `PIF_exVAT = 1 THB` per successful order; `PIF_VAT=0.07` when taxable.
- `account_maintenance = 20 THB/month` for qualifying inactive account, VAT inclusive; balance capture can be partial per policy.
- Net settlement is not fully determinable from these public inputs: `buyer/platform funding receipts + seller proceeds − fee ledger − shipping/fulfillment − optional programs/ads/affiliate − tax/adjustment lines`. Reconcile to actual escrow and adjustments, not this simplified equation.

**Rounding:** Shopee Terms of Service §20.3 states fees including VAT with a fractional satang of **50 satang or more** round up to a whole baht; below 50 satang round down. The term does not explicitly say whether multiple fee types are rounded separately or in aggregate, so examples show raw arithmetic and illustrative per-fee whole-baht posting; match the exact fee lines in settlement. Source: https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/4/article/77241.

## One refund/cancellation example
Order above in standard case is cancelled before completion and accepted under the applicable Shopee policy period: policy 77254 says Shopee may not collect / may waive fees (1),(2),(4)–(7) for cancellation or product return/refund within Shopee Guarantee or other designated period. Thus the illustrative payment+PIF amount should not be treated as a final earned deduction if the qualifying reversal applies. It is **not** evidence that every previously posted line auto-reverses immediately; record original charge and subsequent signed reversal/adjustment separately, verify accepted order state and actual escrow. Account-maintenance fee (3) is not included among this waiver list. Partial return/refund treatment is not publicly specified here.

## Campaign calendar and requirements
No Shopee public complete 2026 campaign calendar or universal campaign fee was found. Treat event dates as campaign dates, not fee-effective dates. Seller Centre event terms control; capture enrollment, price floor/discount, stock, funding split, campaign charge, fulfillment, VAT and refund treatment. The 4 Aug 2026 schedule date is the sale-fee revision, not a campaign calendar.

## Conflicts and unresolved items
1. **Mall sale-fee VAT:** Help Center 77254 says fees (3)-(5), including Mall sale fee, include VAT 7%. Seller Education Hub article 2219 (dated 20 Aug 2026) states that Mall sale and promotional-service rates exclude VAT. These are conflicting official sources; the Hub article is newer and Mall-specific, so provisionally prefer it for Mall schedule interpretation, but block production VAT treatment until current Seller Center schedule/invoice or written Shopee clarification resolves the conflict.
2. **Category-specific rates:** links exposed from 77254 to Thai/English files for both tiers and before/after schedule; numeric rows were not verified/normalized. Rate per category is blocked.
3. **Current campaign calendars, campaign fees, PRIME service rates, free-shipping co-funding, COD, FBS fees, withdrawal charges, exact return-shipping charges:** no complete public numeric source verified. Seller Centre/contract/settlement evidence required.
4. **WHT:** seller profile, fee type and tax documents matter; no one-size-fits-all seller WHT rate/base established.
5. **Order-field arithmetic:** policy says buyer-payable plus shipping + platform PRM + Coins, but exact API field definitions for voucher funding and payment base need verification against escrow response and statement. Avoid duplicate additions.
