# TikTok Shop Thailand — seller fee specification

**Snapshot:** Thailand, THB, 24 October 2026. **Scope:** standard (Non-Mall) and Mall shops. This is a research specification, not a shop quote. **Officially verified** means directly stated in the linked TikTok Seller Center or Open Platform page. If a category table, Seller Center-only rate, or live settlement field was inaccessible, it is marked **UNVERIFIED** rather than inferred.

## Executive summary

There is no defensible universal TikTok Shop take-rate. Per-order deductions can include the seller-tier/category commission, a 3.21% transaction fee, Commerce Growth Fee (usually 6.96% electronics / 8.03% other categories, subject to exceptions and cap), infrastructure fee, optional campaign/program charges, seller-funded promotion, affiliate and ads. Current commission tables are linked in official Seller Center pages, but their individual category rows were not normalized in this research; do not publish a generic total percentage. Baseline formula: `commission + transaction + eligible growth fee + eligible order fee`, plus seller-selected costs. Use Finance settlement for posted values.

## Five-line executive summary

1. **All-in fee range:** no universal range; the current 5 Jul category-commission rows were not normalized in this audit.
2. **Standard fixed/percentage components:** transaction fee 3.21%; Commerce Growth Fee 6.96% electronics / 8.03% other (eligible cases, capped ฿199/item); infrastructure ฿1.07 per eligible delivered order.
3. **Campaign/program costs:** seller-sponsored BCD/Flash can add 3.21% on seller-discounted item price; other 9.9/11.11/12.12/LIVE terms are account/campaign-specific.
4. **Illustrative typical case, not a rate quote:** with hypothetical 7.00% commission on a ฿1,000 item, 8.03% growth fee, 3.21% transaction fee and ฿1.07 infrastructure fee, shown subtotal is ฿183.47 before seller-selected costs.
5. **Production rule:** use actual category table, eligibility and Finance settlement; don't use the hypothetical commission as a current category rate.

## Independent verification note

The detailed transaction-fee article and the current fee overview both repeat the 3.21% transaction rate; the detailed policy controls base and effective date. Numeric 5 July commission rows remain unnormalized. No official Thai fee-rounding rule was found in accessible Seller Center materials; use Finance statement values, not a guessed rounding rule.

## Campaign calendar and funding

The ordinary 9.9, 11.11 and 12.12 periods, Flash Sale and LIVE do not establish one universal fee rate. Accessible official material did **not** establish a universal 2026 campaign entry fee, extra commission, minimum discount, or platform/seller/co-funding split for those events. **Official data not found** for a fixed event fee calendar. Model each campaign from its actual Seller Center invitation and settlement entries. Keep seller price cut, seller coupon, platform coupon, subsidy, affiliate, ads and logistics separate.

## Formula model and worked cases

For one item, before seller shipping, ads and affiliate unless stated:

```text
sale_base = item_price - seller_funded_discount
commission = sale_base * shop_category_rate                 # category rate required
commerce_growth = min(sale_base * eligible_growth_rate, 199)
transaction_fee = (customer_payment + platform_discount) * 0.0321
infrastructure_fee = 1.07 if delivered and eligible
other = seller_program + seller_campaign + seller_affiliate + ads + shipping/adjustments
net_before_COGS = seller receipts + platform reimbursements - deductions - seller-funded discount
```

**Rounding:** no official Thai fee rounding rule was found in accessible Seller Center material. Preserve the raw decimal calculation and use the amount actually posted in the Finance statement.

Rates below are shown tax-inclusive where so stated. Since official current commission table rows were not normalized, the **7.00% commission rate in these illustrations is hypothetical** and is not asserted to be a current category rate. The 2% PayLater program rate is an official example only.

1. **Normal delivered item:** listed ฿1,000; no seller discount; no platform voucher; customer payment ฿1,000; hypothetical category commission 7.00%; non-electronics Commerce Growth 8.03%. Commission ฿70.00 + growth ฿80.30 + transaction `(1,000 + 0) × 3.21% = ฿32.10` + infrastructure ฿1.07 = **฿183.47**, excluding shipping, ads, affiliate and any seller program. Arithmetic independently checked.
2. **Seller discount + platform voucher:** listed ฿1,000; seller discount ฿100; platform voucher ฿100; customer pays ฿800. Commission base ฿900 at hypothetical 7.00% = ฿63.00; growth base ฿900 at 8.03% = ฿72.27; transaction base `(800+100)` = ฿900, fee ฿28.89; infrastructure ฿1.07. **Total ฿165.23**, before other costs. Platform discount excluded from commission/growth base but included in transaction base.
3. **Campaign/program opt-in:** same inputs as case 2 plus seller enrollment in BCD/Flash at 3.21% on the seller-discounted item base: `900 × 3.21% = ฿28.89`; plus PayLater program at illustrative 2% and no refund: `฿800 × 2% × 1.07 = ฿17.12`. Total `165.23 + 28.89 + 17.12` = **฿211.24**. The 2% PayLater rate is an official example, not a universal plan rate; BCD/Flash eligibility, return policy and campaign terms must apply. BCD and Flash cannot stack on the same product.
4. **Return after delivery:** if case 2 is delivered then fully returned, transaction ฿28.89, growth ฿72.27 and infrastructure ฿1.07 remain under the cited rules; commission treatment follows tier/order return policy (Mall policy refunds commission on returned items; verify Non-Mall settlement); seller-fault return shipping may be added. The actual reversal ledger is authoritative.

## API mapping (official schema; fee attribution still needs reconciliation)

- Finance order transaction endpoint: `GET /finance/202501/orders/{order_id}/statement_transactions`; permission `seller.finance.info`. It returns SKU-linked transaction, fee/tax and settlement details. Source: [Get Transactions by Order](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/get-transactions-by-order).
- Finance statement transactions: current documented version **202501**; unsettled transactions **202507**. Confirm exact endpoint paths and all properties in current Open Platform docs before implementation; this research did not verify a full Thai field map for shipping, discounts, affiliate, COD and every seller fee.
- The 22 Sep 2026 additive API fields are: `new_customer_growth_package_fee`, `store_gmv_growth_package_fee`, `target_product_gmv_growth_package_fee`, `auto_post_shoppable_video_commission_fee`; and tax values `new_customer_growth_package_fee_tax`, `store_gmv_growth_package_fee_sales_tax`, `target_product_gmv_growth_package_tax`, `auto_post_shoppable_video_sales_tax`. The notice introduces no request parameter or new permission scope; it is an API-field change, not proof of Thai seller charges. Source: [22 Sep 2026 API change](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2/page/8he1k3et).

## Policy history and confidence

- **1 Jan 2025:** BCD/Flash fee terms and seller-funded installment schedule published (current article states 3.21% BCD/Flash; installment tenor rates above).
- **1 Oct 2025:** infrastructure fee effective at ฿1.07 per delivered order.
- **21 Mar 2026:** transaction-fee base explicitly changed to customer payment plus platform discount.
- **6 May 2026:** Commerce Growth rates changed to 6.96% electronics / 8.03% other, with ฿199 item cap.
- **5 Jul 2026:** current Mall/Non-Mall commission schedule revision effective; full numeric rows still require Seller Center PDF validation.
- **22 Sep 2026:** additive finance API fields; this is an API schema change, not evidence of changed Thai seller tariffs.

**Confirmed:** transaction rate/base/effective date; Commerce Growth rates, base, cap and eligibility; infrastructure amount/assessment; BCD/Flash 3.21% terms; listed installment schedule; VAT/WHT treatment per TikTok explainer; source-defined return rules. **UNVERIFIED/account-specific:** complete Mall/Non-Mall category tables, campaign and program contribution rates, shipping/COD/FBL tariffs, affiliate/ad spend for an individual order, exact rounding, full Thai finance-field map and fee reversals in actual seller statements. Before production, reconcile at least one ordinary, discounted, campaign, canceled and returned order against Finance statement and tax invoice.
